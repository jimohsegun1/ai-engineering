Global Automotive Semiconductor & Supply Chain Network

1. Semiconductor Foundries (Raw Silicon Production)
SiliconCore Fab is an advanced semiconductor fabrication facility located in Dresden, Germany. SiliconCore Fab manufactures two critical silicon components: the SC-9901 high-efficiency powertrain microcontroller and the PC-404 thermal power controller.
TSMC Fab 18 is a large-scale manufacturing plant located in Tainan, Taiwan. TSMC Fab 18 specializes in high-bandwidth sensor silicon, manufacturing the SP-12 optical radar processor and the NPU-Titan neural processing unit.

2. Tier-1 Automotive Component Suppliers
Bosch Mobility, headquartered in Stuttgart, Germany, is a Tier-1 system integrator. Bosch Mobility purchases the SC-9901 microcontroller and PC-404 thermal power controller from SiliconCore Fab. Bosch uses these chips to assemble the MotorControl-X integrated inverter and electric drive system.
Continental Automotive, located in Hanover, Germany, is a Tier-1 safety systems supplier. Continental purchases the SP-12 optical radar processor from TSMC Fab 18. Continental incorporates the SP-12 into the VisionDrive autonomous emergency braking and perception module.
Apex Power Systems, located in Austin, Texas, is an energy storage and power electronics supplier. Apex Power Systems sources the PC-404 thermal power controller directly from SiliconCore Fab to assemble the BMU-300 high-voltage battery management unit.

3. Automotive Manufacturers (OEMs) and Vehicle Assembly
Apex Motors is an electric vehicle manufacturer operating assembly plants in Austin, Texas and Berlin, Germany. Apex Motors manufactures two flagship vehicles: the Apex CyberTruck electric utility pickup and the Apex CyberSedan luxury sedan. Both vehicles require the MotorControl-X drive unit from Bosch Mobility and the BMU-300 battery management unit from Apex Power Systems.
EuroDrive Group is a European automotive group headquartered in Munich, Germany. EuroDrive manufactures the EuroCross electric SUV. The EuroCross SUV requires the MotorControl-X drive system from Bosch Mobility and the VisionDrive perception sensor module from Continental Automotive.

4. Global Logistics Routes and Distribution Hubs
Rhine Logistics Hub in Germany handles inland freight routes connecting the Dresden SiliconCore Fab to the Bosch Mobility assembly plant in Stuttgart.
Rotterdam Container Port in the Netherlands processes transatlantic shipping lines, dispatching maritime container cargo containing PC-404 controllers from Europe to Apex Power Systems in Austin, Texas.

5. Commercial Fleet Customers and Purchase Orders
SwiftLogistics Corporation is a freight carrier headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. SwiftLogistics has placed purchase order ORD-881 for 500 units of the Apex CyberTruck to electrify its regional distribution fleet.
Nordic Fleet Rental is a Scandinavian vehicle leasing conglomerate based in Oslo, Norway. Nordic Fleet Rental has placed purchase order ORD-442 for 300 units of the EuroCross SUV for commercial rental fleets across Northern Europe.
MetroCity Transit is a municipal transport agency in Germany that placed purchase order ORD-105 for 150 units of the Apex CyberSedan for official government service.

6. Failure Cascades and Dependency Mapping
Because automotive assembly requires 100% parts completeness, a supply stoppage at any single node cascades down the entire graph:
- A disruption at SiliconCore Fab in Dresden halts production of SC-9901 and PC-404.
- Without SC-9901 and PC-404, Bosch Mobility cannot assemble MotorControl-X, and Apex Power Systems cannot assemble BMU-300.
- Without MotorControl-X and BMU-300, Apex Motors must pause assembly of CyberTruck and CyberSedan. EuroDrive must pause assembly of EuroCross SUV.
- Consequently, purchase orders ORD-881 (SwiftLogistics), ORD-442 (Nordic Fleet Rental), and ORD-105 (MetroCity Transit) face immediate fulfillment delays.
